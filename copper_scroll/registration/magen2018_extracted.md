Printed p. 79 / PDF p. 70, long tunnel:

> The crag’s shape negated cutting a channel, forcing the aqueduct builders to dig a 12.40 m-long tunnel, bypassing the crag from west to east, and in which a few plaster remains are visible.

Printed p. 79 / PDF p. 70, first fissure:

> About 3.10 m from the western entrance, the cutters encountered a natural crack in the rock that formed a sort of window in the tunnel’s southern wall and bottom.

Printed p. 79 / PDF p. 70, second fissure:

> Another crack in the south, 4 m further on, required that a plastered stone wall be built on the tunnel’s side, the former preserved to a height of 0.47 m.

The intervening repair passage prints a surviving wall height of “0.16 cm.” Visual inspection confirms that unit in the page image. Treat it as a possible printing error; do not silently convert it to metres or use it as a reliable dimension.
