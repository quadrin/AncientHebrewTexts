# Qumran 2002: original survey-data search

28 September 2026. Follow-up to the [registration review](qumran_georeferencing_review.md).

The search recovered four published GPS coordinates from the original team's poster. It did not recover the raw aqueduct survey, CAVEPLOT files, benchmark-coordinate schedule, or a documented transformation from the survey to a geographic datum. The new coordinates remain provisional control candidates. Map geometry and Copper Scroll confidence remain unchanged.

Harry Jol's [research page](https://harry-jol.squarespace.com/international) links to the surviving [2002 project page](https://people.uwec.edu/jolhm/Past_Students/Bob/Subpages/Research/Israel.htm). That page links to [Robert H. Passow's GPS poster](https://people.uwec.edu/jolhm/Past_Students/Bob/Subpages/Research/GPSPoster.htm) and a [joint GPR/GPS poster](https://people.uwec.edu/jolhm/Past_Students/Bob/Subpages/Research/GPRPoster.htm). The pages and their original JPEG assets downloaded successfully even though the web text reader could not retrieve them.

The GPS poster describes a Trimble GPS Pathfinder Pro XR / GPS Pathfinder Office 2.90 workflow, plus Garmin GPSMAP 76S / MapSource. It publishes a–d latitude/longitude values for Tomb 1000. The joint poster repeats that table and associates its fieldwork with 2002. These are two presentations from the same research programme, not independent measurements. Its equipment and correction descriptions do not establish the achieved accuracy of these four observations. Neither inspected poster states their geographic datum or provides a total-station benchmark tie.

The [transcription](tables/qumran_published_gps.csv) retains all four values exactly to five decimal places. Their small cluster cannot validate a transformation across the distant aqueduct. Five decimal places express rounding, not positional accuracy. The source calls them an excavation/GPR area: they must not be relabelled as permanent survey monuments or confidently matched to masonry corners. The smaller latrine-coordinate table is not transcribed because this review could not read all its digits reliably.

The [source manifest](registration/qumran_gps_sources.json) records the image URLs, dimensions and SHA-256 hashes. The source images remain outside the repository. A [2003 conference programme](https://digicoll.lib.berkeley.edu/nanna/record/265548/files/AAG2003__Printed_Program.pdf?registerDownload=1&version=1&withMetadata=0) and [Jol's university-hosted CV](https://www.uwec.edu/sites/default/files/profile-file-uploads/Jol%20CV%202024_0.pdf) independently locate the presentation bibliographically; they add no surveyed control.

Targeted searches covered Qumran/Reeder/CAVEPLOT/benchmark terms, the authors' institutional pages, USF and Wisconsin repository searches, Open Context and Zenodo searches. No matching raw survey download surfaced. The [Zenodo Qumran model](https://zenodo.org/records/10362955) identifies its origin as Objaverse/Sketchfab and supplies a GLB, without a published link to the 2002 survey or its datum. Hartford's [archives page](https://www.hartford.edu/academics/library/archives-and-special-collections/) links to its finding-aid catalogue, which the web reader could not access. Its Qumran holdings remain unverified; this search does not establish that no archive exists.

The next request can now name the exact records: Reeder's July 22–August 4, 2002 aqueduct survey underlying Reeder–Jol 2006 Fig. 4; the total-station observations and adjusted station list; CAVEPLOT inputs/outputs; original drawing; station descriptions and photographs; horizontal and vertical datums; and any connection to Passow's Tomb 1000 a–d GPS observations. Ask which GPS export/correction settings produced the published values and which plotted corners they denote. Confirm reuse permissions before publishing supplied data.

Philip Reeder's [current Duquesne profile](https://www.duq.edu/faculty-and-staff/philip-reeder-phd.php) supplies the first institutional contact route; Harry Jol's [UW–Eau Claire profile](https://www.uwec.edu/profiles/jolhm) supplies a second. Their authorship makes them relevant enquiries; possession of the original files is not confirmed. **A request was sent to both on 29 September 2026** (F8.6); no reply yet.
