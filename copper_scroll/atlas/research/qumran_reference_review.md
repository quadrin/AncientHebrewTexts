**Inference from this comparison:** Qumran remains a plausible locality for a group of water-system descriptions, but no new independent feature match has been demonstrated. The reservoir-to-channel link becomes weaker when the Shallum reading is retained. No continuous route through entries 20–24 has been established, and no confidence percentage is assigned.
