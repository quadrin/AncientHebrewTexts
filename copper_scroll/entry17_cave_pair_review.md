## What the coordinates allow us to test

The nominal V/49 and Cave 38 points are about 79 m apart. V/49's point lies on the northern boundary of Cave 38's reported range. That overlap makes it unsafe to treat the nominal points as measured entrances. It does not establish that the caves overlap physically, share an entrance or form the pair described by Eisenberg.

For a sensitivity check only, hold V/49's catalogue point fixed and allow the second anchor to occupy the whole reported Cave 38 rectangle. Planimetric separation then ranges from 0 to about 180 m. Those limits describe the supplied rectangle, not positional confidence, actual cave dimensions or walking distance. Uncertainty in V/49's own anchor remains additional. A midpoint derived from these inputs would therefore give unsupported precision.

Cave 38 is a **neighbour-identity check**, with no established equivalence to Eisenberg's neighbouring Second Temple dwelling cave. Cave numbers 42, 49 and 38 belong to records that require a numbering crosswalk. Numerical proximity cannot supply that crosswalk. Caves 13 and 20 provide named alternatives for checking the original map; the catalogue alone does not establish which entrances adjoin V/49. The register is a selected research shortlist, not an exhaustive cave inventory.

## Text and occupation tests

This comparison remains conditional on Achor = Wadi Nuweiʿimeh and Puech's cavity/chamber reading of IV 6–8 (2006, pp. 186–187). The trees and buildings readings require different feature inventories. “Midway” supplies no distance between the two landmarks; the depth instruction supplies no horizontal radius.

The recovered Phase 5 extraction cites Eisenberg's Hebrew p. 120 for V/49's ancient use and the neighbouring dwelling cave. That extraction has not been independently rechecked against the original report this turn. A broad Hellenistic/Roman attribution cannot establish simultaneous use of two particular entrances. The necessary chronological test concerns both the surviving openings and the intervening surface during the chosen Copper Scroll dating model.

Once the original pair is identified, compare entrance-centre, chamber-centre and accessible-route midpoint models. A straight line through the cliff is geometrically calculable but may fail to describe an accessible surface. Require the published section or terrain evidence before selecting one of those models. A model requiring a modern cut, collapsed ledge or an undated filled void should lose support.

## ʿAtiqot 41 recovered — 29 September 2026

The complete Hebrew issue has now been read: Eisenberg's V/49 report, Aronshtam's Region V survey, the index and the 1993 map. Page-level notes, quotations and computations are in [the extraction](registration/atiqot41_region_v_extracted.md).

**Result: the neighbouring dwelling cave is V/48, not Cave 38.** Eisenberg names it on p. 120: "This dwelling cave (No. V/48), adjacent to Cave V/49, was hewn beside a path that connected the two caves." He dates its cutting to the Second Temple period without giving evidence.

**V/48, as Aronshtam describes it (p. 94)**

- grid 19040/14365, in the middle of the cliff;
- a hewn row of three rooms parallel to the cliff, with a plastered cistern and a collapsed façade;
- only two non-indicative sherds;
- no plan.

**The catalogue's "Cave 38" is a different cave, V/38.** Its record carries no individual grid point. The Greenberg–Keinan point is the centre of the V/30–47 group box (19025–40/14355–70). The 79 m figure above therefore measures nothing and is withdrawn. The catalogue's "Quruntul Cave 42" is V/49's 1993 field number (Index p. 260).

**V/49 (pp. 105–106)**

- Its two mouths face north (Plan 1, p. 106, the only plan and section).
- It lies in the upper cliff within a small, hidden gully (Aronshtam p. 94).

**Spacing.** By the printed 50 m grid, V/48 lies about 71 m south-east of V/49. The rounding allows 0 to about 141 m. The labelled cliff photographs (Aronshtam Figs. 1–2, pp. 91, 93) put V/38 and V/28–29 between them, so "adjacent" is loose. No midpoint is drawn:

- neither V/48 nor V/38 has a published plan or measured entrance;
- the path's route is not described;
- p. 105 speaks of a path "prepared by the occupants of Cave V/50". This is either the same cliff path or a slip for V/48.

**Candidate effect.** The entry 17 feature-pair lead becomes **V/49 + V/48**. The pair's identity is well supported; its spacing, path and contemporaneous use are not. V/28 (Early Roman sherds) and V/29 are the nearest labelled openings and remain alternatives if "two cavities" is not restricted to Eisenberg's pair. The test stays conditional on Achor = Wadi Nuweiʿimeh and on Puech's cavity reading of IV 6. The plate check could not settle IV 6: see [plate_check.md](plate_check.md). Atlas geometry and confidence labels are unchanged.

**Next.** A field or drone view of the V/48–V/49 path and both entrances, or the unpublished V/38/V/48 plans, would allow the midpoint models to be tested.
