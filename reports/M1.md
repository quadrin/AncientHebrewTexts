# M1 Pairing

Date: 2026-09-24. Scripts (run from the repo root after M0): `src/pair.py` → `src/pair_check.py` → `src/pair_stats.py`. Output: `data/m1/pairs.json` (local only: one record per usable image, with its category, its links and, for usable pairs, the ETCBC lines) and `reports/M1_summary.json`.

## Summary

- **4,243 clean recto pairs, 99,533 preserved letters, 399 manuscripts.** Parchment: 3,160 pairs, 90,975 letters. Papyrus: 1,083 pairs, 8,558 letters.
- 1,916 of these pairs have 10 or more letters, and they hold 88,665 letters.
- Ink on the image agrees with the letter count: Spearman 0.68 on clean recto pairs, 0.71 on parchment.
- The fallback join on fragment number, which the brief proposed, does not work. Do not use it.

## Join sources

1. **SQE catalogue matches** (from M0). This is the main source. Links: `exact` 4,365, `subsiglum` 94, `side_swapped` 7 (counted on one-to-one training pairs).
2. **SQE artefacts** as a flagged fallback, for images with no catalogue match. Only artefacts that carry an edition name (`Frg. N`, `Col. N`) are used, because many carry IAA labels such as `1-1` or `N/A`. On images that have both links, a named artefact agrees with the catalogue match in 93% of cases, and most of the rest name a piece inside the same join (`f8` inside `f6_10`). The fallback adds 235 pairs (about 3,300 letters), and their ink agreement is weaker (Spearman 0.39).
3. **Manuscript + fragment number (rejected).** On 4,864 SQE-confirmed pairs, the Leon Levy `frag_num` (the IAA number of the piece on its plate) is the same as the edition fragment number in only 7% of cases, which is chance level. They are different numbering systems.

## Check 1: by eye

Six random `one_to_one` recto pairs with 25–80 letters were viewed at 1200 px (4Q491 f19, 4Q368 f5, 4Q503 f66, 4Q158 f5, 4Q226 f4, 4Q26a f2). In all six, the number of lines and the legible words agree with the transcription (for example `ואהרון` and `ולאלעזר` on 4Q368 f5; `ויאמר יהוה אל` and `יפרוץ בהמה` on 4Q158 f5). Two of the crops also show the edge of a neighbouring fragment inside the frame.

## Check 2: ink against letters, all 4,702 one-to-one images

Method: the 1200 px rendition (the largest the image server gives; full resolution needs the tile pyramid), with brightness stretched to the 99.9th percentile. Then the parchment mask and letter-sized ink blobs, as in `prior_work/ink.py`. Only the numbers are stored.

| Subset | n | Spearman (all) | Spearman (unflagged) | Clean pairs | Clean letters |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recto | 4,405 | 0.64 | 0.68 | 4,243 | 99,533 |
| Recto parchment | 3,318 | 0.65 | 0.71 | 3,160 | 90,975 |
| Recto papyrus | 1,087 | 0.52 | 0.52 | 1,083 | 8,558 |
| Catalogue `exact` | 4,336 | 0.63 | 0.68 | 4,188 | 95,295 |
| Artefact fallback | 232 | 0.39 | 0.43 | 227 | 3,291 |
| `subsiglum` | 93 | 0.50 | 0.63 | 82 | 2,602 |
| Verso | 263 | 0.47 | 0.47 | 261 | 2,305 |

Flags (164 in total; 34 images failed to download):

- **`too_many_letters_for_area` (50).** The transcription unit is a whole column or a large join (4Q252 col. 2: 410 letters; 4Q219 col. 2: 438; 4Q56 f10_13: 854), but the photo shows one small piece. The pair is "one-to-one" only because the other pieces of that column have no link. The limit is 173 letters per megapixel of full-resolution parchment, which is 3 × the 95th percentile of single-piece pairs (median 28).
- **`letters_without_ink` (113).** Of all 164 flags, 40 are on very large images (scale above 5 px per preview px, for example 11Q5 and XHev/Se5 f1). At 1200 px the letters there are too small to count, so these are measurement failures, not pairing failures. M2 at full resolution will decide.
- **`ink_without_letters` (1).**

Before the brightness stretch, underexposed images (for example 4Q252) showed no parchment, and Spearman was only 0.45.

## Other change: ETCBC word spacing (M0 extract fixed)

ETCBC splits prefixes into separate words (`ו תרומה`, `ה מלחמה`). The extract now takes spaces from the word feature `after`, so lines read as written (`ותרומה`, `המלחמה`). It also keeps unreconstructed lacunae as `#`. The letter counts do not change, so the M0 numbers stand.

## Changes to the plan

- M3 must deal with neighbouring fragments in the frame. Crop to the largest parchment component before line segmentation.
- The 106k letters in `shared` text fragments are the next biggest pool. They need the M3 aligner to decide which lines fall on which piece. Try this only after the one-to-one pairs work.
- M2 should re-run the ink check at full resolution on the 40 large-image flags.
- Drop the fragment-number fallback from the brief.
