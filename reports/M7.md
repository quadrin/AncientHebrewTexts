# M7 Apply

Date: 2026-09-24. Code: `src/prep_batch.py target`, `src/m7_run.py`, `src/m7_queue.py`. Review queue: `reports/M7_queue.md` (top 20). Readings, results and crops stay in `data/m7/` (not committed; crops are Leon Levy images © IAA).

Model: run 2 (M4). Matcher: final M6 version (exact spelling, skippable lines, reconstruction lengths kept in the scroll reference). Text sources in the queue: WLC (openscriptures/morphhb) and ETCBC `dss` (CC BY-NC 4.0, Abegg, Bowley, Cook; converted by Jacobs, Naaijer, Roorda).

## What ran

- **Preprocessing.** All 2,459 recto images of the unidentified sets (4Q9999 plus the other `Unidentified Text` manuscripts, both parchment and papyrus) went through M2; 27 failed (no parchment found or a tiny mask).
- **Reading.** All 2,432 were read. **Letters read per image: median 3** (25th percentile 2, 75th 5, 90th 8, 99th 23). This fits the brief's "3–5 legible letters per line": most pieces are tiny.
- **Matching.** Only images with ≥ 10 letters read were matched, because M5 showed no signal below that. That leaves **167 images, 88 of them from 4Q9999** (then 4Q282 15, WD33 11, 4Q584 7, 4Q562 7, 4Q281 6, 3Q14 5, …). Each was matched with 20 shuffled-reading controls. The p-value comes from the controls in the same read-letter band (its own plus the M5 controls), and E = p × 167.

## Results

- **3 fragments have E < 1.** With 167 searches, about 1 of them is expected by chance.
  1. **B-359582 (4Q9999, IAA plate 202 frag. 19) → Gen 50:15 / ETCBC 4Q12a frag. 1, E = 0.29.** The recogniser reads `ויאמרו לו` / `ור גמלנו`, with the letters מ ר ל ג מ ל נ at probability ≥ 0.8. By eye: line 1 reads `ויאמרו לו` clearly, and line 2 ends in `גמלנו` with the remains of a letter before it. The preserved letters of ETCBC 4Q12a frag. 1 are exactly `ויאמרו לו` (line 1) and `שר גמלנו א` (line 2), Gen 50:15. Leon Levy has no images filed under 4Q12a, and SQE has no 4Q12a edition (M0). So this 4Q9999 photo may be the physical piece published as 4Q12a frag. 1, or a piece of the same text. **This is a lead for a specialist.** The Leon Levy record, the IAA plate 202 inventory and the 4Q12a edition should be compared.
  2. **B-360468 (4Q282) → 2Chr 19:9, E = 0.29.** By eye: a narrow strip with letters cut at the edge. The hit rests on `ליהם`. Probably chance.
  3. **B-483960 (3Q11) → 4Q372 f1:23, E = 0.87.** By eye: clear letters, but the hit rests on the common word `לעשות`. Probably chance.
- **No other fragment reaches E < 1.** The next entries (E 1.2–4) rest on common words (`כאשר`, `אבותיהם`, `ויאמר`) or on noisy readings of faint pieces.

## What changed during M7

- The Gen 50:15 lead exposed a matcher flaw. In the scroll reference, each reconstructed stretch was collapsed into one barrier, so two preserved runs from consecutive lines of one scroll sat too close together for the line-spacing window. The top hit therefore went only to MT. Now each reconstructed letter is kept as a barrier. After the fix, 4Q12a f1:1 and MT Gen 50:15 tie as top hits (30.2). The M5 numbers did not change with this fix (`results_run2_v3`).

## Assessment

- The pipeline works end to end on the target pool. It produced one strong, checkable lead. Its false-hit rate matches its calibration (2 chance-level hits at E < 1 where about 1 is expected).
- **The real limit is the material.** Only 7% of the unidentified images (167 of 2,432) give the ≥ 10 letters the method needs, and only 42 give ≥ 20. For most of 4Q9999, no reader, human or machine, will get enough letters for a statistical placement.
