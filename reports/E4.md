## Summary

- **Many more held-out pieces are now significant, with no new reader and no new data.** The test set is the biblical held-out pieces with 10 or more letters read (55 pieces, run-4 readings).
  - Before E4, 13 of 59 placed their own book at p ≤ 0.05, and none at p ≤ 0.01.
  - Now 21 do at p ≤ 0.05, 16 at p ≤ 0.01, and 10 at p ≤ 0.001.
  - Every piece that passes p ≤ 0.01 with 20 or more letters is in its own book.
- **Three things caused the gain:**
  - clean decoys;
  - a fitted tail for the null distribution;
  - consistent run-4 readings.

  The main fault before was a contaminated null. Real parallels were in the decoy set, and some of them entered through catalogue links to the wrong fragment.
- **Below 20 letters, the gain is small.** For 10–19 letters, 2 of 20 pieces reach p ≤ 0.01. Below 10 letters, still nothing is significant.
- **Rescoring helps at p ≤ 0.05.** Adding the gap to the next-best text and other features raises the count from 21 to 30 (empirical p, same null set). The empirical p cannot go below about 0.01, so the gain at stricter levels is still unmeasured.
- **Book and chapter labels are reliable.** When a label is given, it is right 71 times in 72 on training pieces and 21 times in 21 on the test pieces. It is given only to a minority of pieces: 2 of 20 at 10–19 letters.
- **Summing over line spacings (step 6)** gives the same counts at p ≤ 0.05 and slightly better ones at 0.01 and 0.001 (17 and 12 of 55), with fewer false positives on held-out nulls.
- **Entrapment (step 8)** found no hits in the Mishnah and Tosefta among the passing pieces. This check is weak: that corpus is easier to reject than the target texts (see step 8).
- **The 4Q12a lead is still second in the new queue.** B-359582 now has p = 0.0045, and the posterior places it in Gen 50. But after correction for 1,209 targets its q-value is 1, so the statistic alone no longer supports it. The earlier E = 0.17 came from the old, too-optimistic calibration. The visual full-resolution check (M8) and the specialist check are what count now.
- **E2 step 1:** the SQE letter polygons are harvested and registered to our crops. That gives 2,534 gold letters on 278 pieces.

## Step 1: decoy audit, and the decoy fix

- **What the v2 decoys contained.** The heaviest v2 "decoys" were true parallels:
  - 4Q58 (a copy of Isaiah) matching Isaiah, and 4Q258 (a copy of the Community Rule) matching 1QS;
  - 1Q8 (1QIsa-b) matching Isaiah, and 4Q158 (Reworked Pentateuch) matching Exodus;
  - 4Q321a matching its own text, because the name `4Q321a-190` did not normalise.

  In total, 68 biblical manuscripts were among the decoy sources. Several of these photos are ones the M9 forward audit flagged as showing text other than their link.
- **Why.** A decoy was tested only on its linked fragment's text.
- **v3 rule** (composition names): no biblical composition, a composition with no other copy, no audit flag. This was not enough: 4Q365 (Reworked Pentateuch → Leviticus) and 4Q176 (Tanhumim, which quotes Isaiah) stayed in.
- **v4 rule** (`ms_parallel.py`): the whole manuscript's preserved text is cut into 10-letter skeleton shingles (ו/י removed). A manuscript can supply decoys only if all of these hold:
  - at most 3% of its shingles occur in another document;
  - no shared stretch is longer than 13 skeleton letters;
  - it has at least 20 shingles.

  The v3 rules also still apply. This leaves 1,358 decoy photos from 110 scrolls.
- **What is still in the null.** A few real parallels remain because the copies overlap in little preserved text: 11Q14 ↔ 4Q285, 4Q280 ↔ 1QS 2, 4Q545 ↔ 4Q543, 4Q415 ↔ 4Q418. They make the null heavier, so the calibration is conservative, not optimistic.
- **Resolution floor.** The report expected the ×1.5 length window to hold too few decoys. That is true only at 40+ letters, where 23% of pieces had fewer than 99 decoys. For 10–39 letters there were 185–459.

## A masking bug found and fixed

- **The bug.** The first E4 pass excluded a piece's own manuscript by masking only the start offsets of the chain. A chain of many lines that started in the previous document could still reach the piece's own text through its later lines. Examples: 4Q184 → "4Q183", 11Q14 → "11Q13", and 13 more like them.
- **The fix.** `pmatch.score_array(mask=...)` now sets every line's score to 0 at masked positions before chaining.
- **Scope.** The earlier M5 and M9 runs rebuilt the corpus without the manuscript, so they are not affected.

## Steps 2–4: nulls and statistic

- **Source-excluded nulls.** 384 identified biblical training pieces were searched with every witness of their book masked (the MT book and every scroll copy of it).
- **Statistic.** T = S − ln N, with ln N = ln(1,845,669) + (L − 1) ln 126.
- **Tail fit.** The null comes from decoys of similar length (×1.5):
  - count the islands per search, i.e. local maxima at least 200 letters apart;
  - fit an exponential tail past the 90th percentile of those counts;
  - take p = 1 − exp(−E(T)).
- **Other null models tried** (first E4 pass, `reports/E4.json`):
  - a fixed information bin: too flat a tail at high information, because it mixes long and short readings;
  - an empirical maximum: its p cannot go below about 0.01;
  - a tail fitted on decoys only: 0.6% of held-out decoys at nominal 0.001.

  The model kept is the tail fitted on decoys plus source-excluded nulls.
- **Folds.** Decoy and srcx manuscripts are split into three folds: A fits the null, B trains the rescoring model, C is held out.

**Main run, M5 biblical test pieces:**

| Letters read | Pieces | Top hit in own book | p ≤ 0.05 (own / all) | p ≤ 0.01 | p ≤ 0.001 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3–5 | 36 | 0 | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 |
| 6–9 | 17 | 2 | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 |
| 10–19 | 20 | 14 | 2 / 3 | 2 / 3 | 1 / 1 |
| 20–39 | 15 | 12 | 6 / 6 | 3 / 3 | 2 / 2 |
| 40+ | 20 | 20 | 13 / 13 | 11 / 11 | 7 / 7 |
| **10+ pooled** | **55** | **46** | **21** | **16** | **10** |

**Held-out nulls (fold C) flagged:**

| Null set | p ≤ 0.05 | p ≤ 0.01 | p ≤ 0.001 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decoys only | 0.5% | 0% | 0% |
| Decoys + source-excluded | 1.5% | 0.6% | 0.3% |

The source-excluded nulls pass more often than the decoys. The likely cause: parallel passages elsewhere in the Bible (Kings / Chronicles, doublets in Psalms), which are not pure chance.

**Against the report's targets.**

| Report target | Result | Met? |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 25 of 59 own book at p ≤ 0.05 | 21 of 55 | No |
| ≥ 10 of 59 own book at p ≤ 0.01 | 16 of 55 | Yes |
| Held-out decoys flagged ≤ 6% at α = 0.05 | 0.5% (1.5% with source-excluded nulls) | Yes |
| Held-out decoys flagged ≤ 1.5% at α = 0.01 | 0% (0.6% with source-excluded nulls) | Yes |

## Step 5: rescoring

- **Model.** A logistic model on five features:
  - −log10 p;
  - the gap to the best hit in another text (capped at 50 nats);
  - reading information;
  - lines read;
  - log of letters read.
- **Training data.**
  - Positives: 157 training pieces whose top hit is their own book, from 371 searched with only their own manuscript masked.
  - Negatives: 523 fold-B decoys and source-excluded nulls.
- **Comparison.** T alone and the rescored value were each turned into an empirical p with the same 340 fold-C nulls.
  - Own book at p ≤ 0.05: 21 of 55 with T alone, **30 of 55** rescored.
  - At p ≤ 0.01: 0 for both. With about 100 nulls per length window, the empirical p cannot go lower, so this does not measure the gain at stricter levels.
- **Next.** A tail fit for the rescored value.

## Step 6: sum over line spacings

- **Method.** `pmatch.score_array(forward=True)` replaces the maximum over the 126 spacings with a windowed log-sum-exp minus ln 126. The chained score stays a likelihood ratio with mean 1, and ln N loses its (L − 1) ln 126 term. It is computed in blocks and matches a direct computation to 4e-15.
- **Result** (same pieces and null model):

| Scoring | Own book, 10+ letters, at p ≤ 0.05 / 0.01 / 0.001 | Held-out nulls flagged at the same levels |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum over spacings | 21 / 16 / 10 | 1.5% / 0.6% / 0.3% |
| Sum over spacings | 21 / 17 / 12 | 0.9% / 0.3% / 0.3% |

- **Verdict.** A small, consistent gain. It is worth making the default after a check on the M7 queue.

## Step 7: lowest-level assignment

- **Book posterior.** P_book = 1 / (1 + e^−gap + odds₀), with odds₀ = p/(1 − p) × (1 − π)/π and π = 0.5.
- **Chapter posterior.** The same form over the top MT chapters.
- **Rule.** A piece gets a label when the posterior is at least 0.95.
- **Scroll copies.** They are mapped to their books with ETCBC's `book` feature (`data/e4/scroll_books.json`). Without that map, a scroll copy such as 4Q12a counted as a text separate from its MT book. That set the gap of B-359582 to 0.

| Set | Labelled | Right | Wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| ownx (source present) | 72 of 371 | 69 to the chapter, 2 to the book | 1 |
| Fold-C nulls (no source) | 3 of 340 | — | 3 |
| M5 test pieces | 21 of 184 | 17 to the chapter, 4 to the book | 0 |

- **Coverage.** Among the 10–19-letter test pieces, 2 of 20 get a label. The report's target was 50% at 95% precision, so the labels are right when given but are given rarely.

## Step 8: entrapment

- **The corpus** (`entrap_ref.py`):
  - the Mishnah (63 tractates) and Tosefta (Vilna edition, 61 tractates) from the Sefaria export, 1.95M letters (r = 1.03);
  - 54k letters blanked where they share a 10-letter skeleton with the reference;
  - Sefaria gives no licence field for these files, so they stay local.
- **The test.** In the same search, T_comb = max(T, T_en) − ln(1 + r).
- **Result.** Among the pieces that pass, none had its best hit in the entrapment corpus: M5 test 0 of 22, held-out nulls 0 of 5, ownx 0 of 79 at p ≤ 0.05. So the FDP upper bound is 0 at every level.
- **Caveat.** Mishnaic Hebrew with its shared stretches removed is an easy corpus to reject. It shows that the matcher does not place pieces in an arbitrary text of the same size. It does not show that the false-discovery rate among Qumran-like texts is 0. A fairer entrapment corpus would be Hebrew of the same period and register that no Qumran scroll can come from, and none exists in reach.
- **Queue.** Among the 1,209 targets, the entrapment corpus beats the reference in 85, all with low scores.

## E2 step 1: SQE letter polygons

- **Harvest** (`sqe_rois.py`). The polygons come from `/editions/{id}/script-lines`: 4,050 polygons, 3,403 letters, 80 editions and 351 imaged objects, as the report found.
  - 40 polygons belong to reconstructed signs.
  - 203 are marked "incomplete and not clear", i.e. partial letters.
  - `script-lines` gives each attribute as one string (e.g. `sign_type_LETTER`), not as a key/value pair.
- **Join.** 325 of 352 imaged object/side pairs join to a Leon Levy photo; 294 have an M2 crop.
- **Registration** (`roi_register.py`):
  1. Fetch the SQE IR image at 1/8 scale. The IIIF server serves only its listed sizes: 225, 451 or 902 px wide, and 256 px tiles.
  2. Find a SIFT + RANSAC similarity transform to our half-resolution Leon Levy image.
  3. Apply it, then our M2 deskew and crop offset.
- **Registration results.**
  - 288 of 294 pieces register.
  - 278 have scale 1.00 and at least 30 inliers. Of those, 248 are at 0° and 29 are Leon Levy photos turned by ±90°.
  - The median share of mapped letter boxes that fall on ink is 1.0, against 0.5 when the same boxes are shifted.
  - Overlays on 5 pieces, checked by eye, put the boxes on the letters.
- **Gold letters.** 2,534 in our crop frame: train 1,837, test 556, validation 13, outside the split 128.
  - 130 are partial letters.
  - 2,485 fall inside an M2 line band.
- **Limit.** SQE annotated only some lines on each piece, so a letter without a box does not mean no letter.
- **What stays local.** The mapped coordinates are in `data/sqe_rois/` (local), and so are the SQE images.

## What changes in the plan

1. **Make the E4 calibration the standard.** It uses the v4 decoys plus source-excluded nulls, the fitted tail and run-4 readings. The old E-values in M7 and M8 are too optimistic and should not be quoted.
2. **Queue.** Mask each target set's own transcription, and report own-set hits as catalogue fixes.
3. **Decide on the sum over line spacings.** It is slightly better on the held-out test; re-run the queue with it and make it the default if the queue does not get worse.
4. **Rescoring.** Fit a tail for the rescored value so that it can give p below 0.01; at p ≤ 0.05 it already gains 9 pieces.
5. **E2 step 2 is next.** Measure `align.py` and `align_self.py` against the 1,837 training gold letters: band assignment, coverage and label errors.
6. **The limit stays below 10–15 letters.** No calibration change makes a 3-letter scrap significant.

## Follow-up (2026-09-26): own-set masking, sum over spacings in the queue, rescored tail, E2 step 2

### Queue with each set's own transcription masked

- **Two searches per target.** Targets whose set has an ETCBC transcription of its own (e.g. 4Q562, 4Q584) are searched twice:
  - unmasked, to record a hit in the own transcription;
  - with the own transcription masked, for the queue.
- **Catalogue-fix list.** Photos whose best unmasked hit is their own set go to a separate list at the end of `reports/E4_queue.md`. There are 3; 1 has p ≤ 0.01: B-361671 → 4Q562 frag. 1, p = 6e-6. That is a photo without its catalogue link, not an identification.
- **The masked queue.** B-359582 (the 4Q12a lead) is now rank 1. No target passes q ≤ 0.2 on the T-based p.

### Rescored score with a fitted tail

- **Folds.** The nulls outside fold A are split by manuscript three ways: 349 train the logistic model, 239 fit the tail of the rescored value (exponential past the 80th percentile, length window ×1.5), and 275 are held out.
- **Result** (M5 test pieces, 10+ letters, own book; held-out nulls flagged on the same fold):

| Score | p ≤ 0.05 | p ≤ 0.01 | p ≤ 0.001 | Held-out nulls flagged (0.05 / 0.01 / 0.001) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T (tail p) | 21 | 16 | 10 | 2.6% / 2.2% / 0.7% |
| Rescored (tail p) | **31** | **26** | **12** | 3.3% / 1.8% / 0.4% |

- **Verdict.** Rescoring adds 10 held-out pieces at p ≤ 0.01 at about the same false-positive rate.
- **Caveat.** Both scores flag somewhat more than nominal on this fold, which includes the source-excluded nulls (parallel passages).
- **In the queue** (column "p rescored"):
  - B-359582: p = 2e-7, which is E ≈ 0.0002 over 1,209 targets.
  - That p is extrapolated far past the 239 tail nulls, and the held-out check shows the tail is 3–4 times too optimistic at 0.001. Even if it were 100 times too optimistic, E ≈ 0.02.
  - Next in the queue:
    - B-511088 (9 letters, 4Q252) p = 0.003;
    - B-511164 (11 letters, Ezek 40:46) p = 0.004;
    - B-494995 (19 letters, 4Q184) p = 0.008.

    Chance alone gives about 12 targets at p ≤ 0.01, so these three are what chance predicts.

### E2 step 2: M2 bands and M3 aligners against the gold letters (`src/e2_align_eval.py`, `reports/E2_align_eval.json`)

- **The SQE line field cannot be trusted.** Checked by eye on B-358099: the letter identities and positions are right, but SQE files letters of four physical lines under line "4". So physical lines are rebuilt from the gold boxes, clustered by vertical centre.
- **Segmentation (M2).**
  - 417 of 423 physical gold lines (98.6%) lie inside one M2 band.
  - Between 23 and 85 of 443 bands (5–19%) hold letters of two gold lines; the range depends on the clustering threshold.
- **Line choice.** For each labelled band with 3 or more gold letters, the gold letters, read right to left, are matched against every ETCBC line of the fragment. The aligner is right when its label's line matches best.

| Aligner | Train | Test | Self-aligned (train) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Width alignment (M3, tiers A/B) | 50 of 51 bands right | 18 of 27 right | — |
| Self-alignment (tier S) | — | — | 39 of 40 right |

- **Test misses.** All 9 are one photo: B-368240 (4Q54 frag. 5, tier B). The width alignment shifted every band by one line, so each label names the next line. Those labels are in the M4 test set, so they inflate its error rate.
- **Label text.** When the line is right, the median edit rate of the gold letters inside the label is 0 (width) and 0.05 (self).
- **What changes.**
  - M3 needs a check against whole-photo shifts: compare each band's reading with the neighbouring lines' text as well as its own.
  - B-368240 should be re-labelled or dropped from the test set.
