# M3 Weak line labels (first pass)

Date: 2026-09-24. Code: `src/align.py`, `src/line_sheet.py` (inspection sheets). Line crops and the manifest stay in `data/m3/` (not committed). Numbers: `reports/M3_summary.json`.

This is the first pass, before any training. The brief's second step, cleaning by CTC loss after a first training round, follows M4.

## Method

- **Labels.** Each transcription line becomes a visible label. Only preserved letters (`.` and `u`) are kept, and the line is trimmed to its preserved span. Word spaces are kept, and final forms stay as written (27 classes plus space). If a span contains reconstructed letters or a lacuna (a hole in the image), the line is marked `has_gap`.
- **Scale.** Character width per line pitch was calibrated on fragments where the counts agree: median 0.284 (IQR 0.233–0.347, n = 3,075 lines).
- **Alignment.** A monotonic dynamic program (top to bottom) over image bands and transcription lines, with three moves:
  - *match*: the cost is the width misfit |log(ink width / expected width)| plus 1.5 × |band spacing in pitches − line-number difference|;
  - *skip a band*: cost 1.0, or 0.4 for a weak band;
  - *skip a transcription line*: cost 1.2, or 0.3 for a line with 2 letters or fewer.
- **Tiers.**
  - **A** (the brief's rule): the band count equals the transcription line count, every line is matched, and every width misfit is below 0.5 (a factor of 1.65).
  - **B**: aligned with skips, and the matched line's width misfit is below 0.5.
  - Lines above the misfit limit are dropped.
- **Crops.** The band's ink extent ± 12 px, and ±0.7 pitch around the profile peak. The first window (−0.75/+0.45 pitch) cut off the bottoms of letters.

## Results

Input: 3,159 fragments, 90,941 preserved letters.

| | Lines | Letters | Letters without a gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier A | 2,318 | 17,861 | 15,328 |
| Tier B | 4,403 | 49,551 | 37,975 |
| **A + B** | **6,721** | **67,412** | 53,303 |
| Dropped (misfit) | 1,832 | | |

- The surviving lines come from 2,642 fragments of 361 manuscripts: Herodian 3,488 lines, Hasmonean 2,131, no period given 819, other 283.
- Letters per line: median 7 (10th percentile 3, 90th percentile 21). 17% of the label letters are marked uncertain by the editors.
- All 27 letter forms occur. The rarest are ף (103) and ץ (166); the commonest are ו (8,185) and י (6,929).

**Inspection by eye.** 32 random lines were shown next to their labels (16 from each tier; the first two sheets had a display bug, then two new sheets were drawn). Clearly the same text: A `מעליכם ונעלך לו`, `ובחסדיכ`, `בל יעלנה`, `יאכלו`; B `בתמים דרך עם קץ`, `ימים רבים ושמ`, `מעשיו כמול`, `לטרוף וככפיר`, `די קודמוהי`, and others. In the second pair of sheets, 14 of 16 clearly match and 2 are too faint to judge. No line was clearly mislabelled. Some crops include parts of the neighbouring lines, which is normal for line recognisers.

## Changes to the plan

- Train M4 on A + B (67k letters) with a sample weight per tier, instead of A alone (18k letters is thin for 27 classes).
- Keep the `has_gap` lines out of the first round, because CTC cannot see the hole in the image, and add them back after cleaning.
- Reverse the labels for training: Hebrew runs right to left, and the network reads the image left to right.
- After the first training round: compute a per-line CTC loss, drop the worst lines (the brief's cleaning step), realign the fragments with the recogniser's own reading, and report how many letters survive.
